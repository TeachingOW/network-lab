Where bulkapi’s CPU time goes

Single requests are bound by the kernel. Our parser, router and handler together cost under 1 µs.

Bulk requests spend most of their time in our own code. That is where optimising pays.

2 / 30

Before changing anything, this is the split between time in our own code and time in the kernel, per request, with one worker. For a plain GET the kernel takes 91 percent, so tuning the parser or the router can gain at most a few percent. Bulk requests are different: a bulk get of 100 items spends 39 microseconds in our code against 16 in the kernel. A function-level profile was not available on this machine, so the ranking of ideas rests on this split and on reading the code.
