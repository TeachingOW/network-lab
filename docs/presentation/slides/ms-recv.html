Optimisation 1 · implemented

Multishot receive

What changed. One receive is armed per connection and keeps delivering data into a shared ring of buffers. Before, a receive was submitted again after every request.

Result. Cost per request stays flat as connections grow. It is cheaper than single-shot at 2,000 connections and dearer at 256.

The first implemented change is a second io_uring backend, selected with backend uring-ms. It uses io_uring_prep_recv_multishot from liburing 2.15: one submission per connection, and the kernel posts a completion for each chunk of data into a buffer taken from a shared ring of 1,024 buffers of 16 kilobytes. The measured effect is a trade-off, not a plain win. Multishot costs about 15 microseconds per request whatever the connection count. Single-shot costs about 9 at 256 connections but 27 at 2,000. So multishot is the backend for thousands of connections and single-shot for a few hundred or fewer. Why single-shot degrades with connection count has not been established.
